# Happily Ever Planner: an Unofficial Companion for Fabledom

Lightweight PWA built to help players plan and optimize city layouts for Fabledom. Capable of importing all map terrain and resource deposits from a game-generated save-game file, planning/mapping all buildings and desirability effects, and saving full kingdom playthroughs and terrain maps. 

*Current version: 0.3.0*

Thank you for taking the time to visit the page and give this project a try. I genuinely appreciate your interest, and I hope this tool helps you plan your kingdom just as much as it has helped me!

## Getting Started: Importing A Terrain Map from the Game
Because this runs entirely in your browser, the app cannot automatically scrape your computer for save files. You'll need to locate your Fabledom .SAVEGAME folder and upload the file manually.

**How to find your Fabledom saves on Windows:**
1. Open File Explorer.
2. Navigate to: C:\Users\ [Your Username] \AppData\LocalLow\Grenaa Games\Fabledom\Saves
3. The AppData folder is hidden by default. If you can't see it, click "View" at the top of File Explorer and check "Hidden items" to enable viewing it. 

There are also in-app instructions to help you find the folder when you click on the "Import Save File" button.

## Install to Desktop
This is a Progressive Web App (PWA), which means you can install it directly to your computer like a native program. It will give you a desktop shortcut, an icon in your app tray, and it will run in its own clean window without the browser search bar and navigation buttons. **Moreover, it stores a local cache of its core files so it can run completely offline, with no internet connection.** It's still using your browser to run, and it's still completely sandboxed, without any access to your files besides what you explicitly give it if/when you point it to your SaveData folder or a preferred location to store your backups and saved realms.

**To install on Chrome/Edge:**
Look for and click the "Install App" icon on the right side of your browser's URL address bar. On Chrome it usually looks like a computer screen with a downward arrow. On Edge, it's three squares and a plus sign. Alternatively, click your browser's menu button (three dots) and select "Install Happily Ever Planner."

**To install on Firefox:** 
Open your Firefox Settings from the browser menu in the top right corner. Click on "Firefox Labs" in the left panel and enable the "Add sites to your taskbar" feature. Once it's enabled, you should see an installation icon on the right side of your URL address bar just like Chrome & Edge.

It also runs just fine in the browser, with all the same functionality, if you prefer that! It just doesn't look quite as nice.

## A Note on AI Assistance
Full disclosure up front: I built this app with the help of AI. As a longtime member of the gaming community, I share the community's concerns about the use of AI by large companies to replace the disciplined, creative efforts of talented writers, artists, and voice actors with lazy, poor quality shortcuts just to save a few dollars. 

The Happily Ever Planner doesn't have any of that. I’m just a 40-year-old gamer dad with no coding experience who wanted a way to blueprint my Fabledom kingdom and test out layouts before committing to them in the game. Figuring out how to build that in my spare time was an impossible stretch, so I leaned heavily on AI tools like Gemini to bridge my coding gaps, act as my tutor, and bring my artistic vision to life. With that massive assist, I managed to build the exact planning tool I wished existed, and I've spent many hours ensuring the end result is a quality product, not hastily-generated slop. Now that it gets the job done for my own realm, I'm happy to share it with anyone else looking for the same thing, and I hope you find it as useful as I have!
